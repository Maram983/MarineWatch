About MarineWatch

Helping Protect Marine Ecosystems

MarineWatch brings together divers, volunteers and environmental authorities to improve marine conservation through pollution reporting and community participation.

1

Report Pollution

Certified divers can submit pollution reports with photos, location, and environmental details directly from the field.

2

Review Reports

Administrators review submitted reports before publishing them on the Marine Map.

3

Volunteer Activities

Volunteers can participate in cleanup activities and help protect affected marine areas.

How It Works

Simple Reporting Process

MarineWatch follows a simple workflow from reporting pollution to organizing cleanup activities.

1
Submit Report

A certified diver submits a pollution report with its location and description.

2
Review

The administrator checks the report and decides whether to approve or reject it.

3
Marine Map

Approved reports become visible on the Marine Map to help identify polluted areas.

4
Cleanup Activities

Volunteers join environmental activities to help protect and restore marine ecosystems.
